ProjHub MCP is a cross-tool visibility and utility-tool safety task, not a single-tool bug hunt.
The first calibration was over-hardened: many items were Type A because they combined too many callers, surfaces, or state invariants. This revision keeps the high-signal patterns that worked and rewrites hard or inverted items into smaller, judge-proof chains.

Design strategy:
- keep the proven B-style checks: ping payloads with benign control, comment visibility with public control, and full-response assertion quality;
- convert over-broad regression items into explicit one-flow anti-overblock checks;
- shorten hard chains into one or two exact tool checks with captured IDs or named sentinels;
- remove the inverted lifecycle rubric and replace it with simpler product-regression checks;
- keep only a small number of deliberately hard security checks.
