The Aspen pipeline grades a frontier model on its ability to author a
hidden test suite for seeded information-exposure and access-control
surfaces in the `mcp_vault` tool substrate. The agent inspects a small
Python MCP (tool) codebase with seeded documents and sentinel markers,
identifies call sites that expose private fields (preview/export/audit),
and writes pytest tests that (a) catch ordinary-leak exposures and (b)
assert that legitimate participant and admin flows remain allowed.

The 11 rubric items decompose into three structural groups: read-side
leak and response-shaping coverage (5 criteria), admin and cross-org
legitimate-access guards (3 criteria), and serialization/test-quality
checks (3 criteria). Severity weights are `critical=4, major=3,
minor=2, nitpick=1` producing arithmetic: `1*4 + 7*3 + 2*2 + 1*1 = 30`
(`rubric_max_score = 30`). The reasoning and group counts above are
derived directly from `aspen_mcpvault_security_002/task_config.json`.
