ProjNeo is a visibility-and-exposure task, not a single-endpoint bug hunt.
Ordinary participants can cross team boundaries through core reads, child-resource lookups,
delegated-access state, operational surfaces, summary-style endpoints, and crafted-input
paths, while non-privileged views also expose privileged metadata through inconsistent
response shaping.

This Go substrate keeps the successful calibration style: seeded state, explicit actors,
sentinel markers, central project/task gates, central serializers, and multiple primary,
secondary, discovery, operational, and unsafe-input surfaces. The suite should enforce the
dual contract: catch cross-boundary access, secondary-path redaction failures, delegated
scope drift, unsafe input handling, predictable tokens, verbose errors, and operational
leaks without overblocking legitimate same-team flows.

Rubric decomposition mirrors the successful Go task: 5 critical merged chains, 8 major
supporting items, 1 minor repeatability item, and 1 nitpick quality item.
Severity arithmetic: 4x5 + 8x3 + 1x2 + 1x1 = 47.
